## Problem

Industrial and energy applications needed backend services with clearer domain boundaries and less synchronous coupling across distributed components.

## Solution

I developed Java, Spring Boot, and Go microservices and REST APIs for Siemens Xcelerator and Energy projects IAH, ICMA, and OAM. I implemented Kafka and RabbitMQ integrations and worked on containerized and serverless AWS deployments. I used domain-driven and API-first design to shape interfaces and service boundaries.

## Impact

Asynchronous communication decoupled services. SQL tuning, distributed caching, JVM tuning, and architectural refactoring improved performance and maintainability.
